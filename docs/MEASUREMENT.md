# Longitudinal self-experiment and application-efficiency measurements

Track what actually happens, but do not invent the past. The original Day1 work predates this data model, and some early times, baselines and technical states were not measured. Those are **UNKNOWN / retrospective** rather than silently assigned values.

## Baseline

`framework/BASELINE.md` records source-supported background, existing familiarity, limits and areas needing demonstration. Claims of 'tested' technical work are backed by actual current artifacts; they are not automatically retroactive descriptions of Day 1 expertise.

## Minimum recurring measurements

- Per attempt and target: date selected, preparation started, submitted, actual outcome, follow-up; see `applications.csv` and `application_events.csv`.
- Time invested by activity where known: research, coding, testing, documentation, application preparation, publishing; see `time_log.csv`.
- Per Sprint: previously missing evidence, concrete work produced, tests, skills, reused assets and observed limitation.
- Skills: states and evidence links; changes are dated and are not global mastery declarations.
- Reuse: which code, prompt, template, research sources and application steps carry forward from previous attempts.
- Outcome: interviews, offers, declines, publications, feedback (only with observed evidence).
- Industry signals: source-backed role requirements and hiring patterns in context, not fabricated aggregate percentages.

## Compare later attempts

At Attempt #002 onward, ask whether preparation time changed, earlier tools were reused, scope was reduced, capability evidence deepened and job outcomes changed. Report confounding circumstances and small sample limits; one application does not prove that ProofLoop caused an improvement.
