# Evidence, skills and claim boundaries

Distinguish:

1. **Employer requirement**: original job wording and observation date; cite source ID and required/preferred/alternative context.
2. **Self-reported experience**: résumé claim with its actual scope; not independently verified.
3. **Project evidence**: inspectable code, test result, data artifact or demo, with AI assistance acknowledged.
4. **Unresolved gap**: distinguish missing evidence of a real ability, missing ability, uncertainty and specialized experience not established.

**Research states:** DOCUMENTED, CORROBORATED, REPORTED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN, SUPERSEDED. Each major claim should retain sources, time context, and limitations. See Observatory `claims.csv` and `relationships.csv`.

**Skill progression:** UNEXPLORED, LEARNING, EXPERIMENTED, APPLIED, TESTED, DOCUMENTED, REPEATED, PROVEN. These states describe evidence, not an automatic hierarchy of professional seniority. *TESTED* requires a specific test; *PROVEN* requires appropriately substantial evidence. A skill may have multiple independent proofs and limitations; never infer enterprise seniority from an AI-assisted learning project.

`framework/ledgers/requirements.csv` contains source-linked employer requirements; `evidence.csv` contains inspectable claim/artifact sources; `proof_graph.csv` links the two (and may record explicitly unsupported requirements); `skills.csv` references capabilities and their current evidence limitations. The purpose is honest, traceable reasoning—not numerical fictional fit or hire probabilities.
